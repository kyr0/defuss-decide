## v0.2 task adapter ports

`src/spans.ts` and `src/task-format.ts` port the word alignment, routing and span decoding logic from `vela3_schema.py` (vela3-fast, Apache-2.0) and `probe_lib.py`, `span_decode_v2.py`, `uvb_core.py`, `vela3v_encoder.py` (vela3-fast-vision package, MIT; its upstream components retain their original licenses). Ports deliberately reject long inputs instead of implementing truncation/window aggregation. The derived tests record outputs of the pinned original implementations. See the bundled license texts and upstream source links in `documentation/ADAPTER_VALIDATION.md`.

## Fixtures and assets

- `DejaVuSans.ttf` (DejaVu Fonts; Bitstream Vera and Arev licenses, DejaVu changes public domain) renders the OCR sample image in `python/export_tasks.py`. Its license is embedded in the font and copied to `documentation/licenses/DejaVu-Fonts-LICENSE.txt`.
- `tests/fixtures/bundles/tokenizer-*.json` and `tokenizer_config-*.json` are the tokenizers of the pinned checkpoints (vela3-fast: Apache-2.0; vela3-fast-vision: MIT and Apache-2.0). The fixture `fixtures.json` files are recorded from the pinned original implementations, the PNG images are rendered by the exporters, and the tiny ONNX graphs are generated by `scripts/make-fixture-bundles.py` and carry no upstream weights.
- The website loads defuss-shadcn (MIT), ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and Tokenizers.js (Apache-2.0) from jsDelivr at pinned versions; it does not redistribute them.
- `docs/assets/vendor/highlight.js` bundles Shiki and its JSON grammar and GitHub themes (MIT), built from the installed packages by `scripts/docs.ts`.
- The page's icons use path data from Lucide (ISC); the region icon adds a dot to Lucide's scan frame.
- The key handling of the page's state and question editors (`docs/assets/site.js`: Tab and Shift+Tab indent, Enter keeps the indentation, Escape then Tab leaves) is adapted from defuss-shadcn's code-example component (MIT); the drop zone is its file-input component, loaded with the rest of defuss-shadcn.
- `docs/assets/aron-homberg.jpg` is the maintainer's portrait for the footer, resized to 400 px from the defuss-vae website.
- `docs/assets/samples/synthetic/` holds synthetic sample images supplied by the maintainer, resized to at most 1,024 px and stored as WebP.
